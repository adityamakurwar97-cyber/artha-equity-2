## Material corrections

The source reports net March 2026 shares of 4,046,940,812. Gross shares are 4,055,591,723 and treasury shares 8,650,911. Gross minus treasury equals reported net. The inherited model deducted treasury twice. June's separately retained net observation is 4,049,645,811, published July 23. The March model uses March shares. Period-end employee-award dilution remains unavailable.

Infosys FY26 pre-exception EBIT is ₹37,378 crore; reported EBIT after the ₹1,289 crore Labour Codes item is ₹36,089 crore. The overview/forecast uses pre-exception EBIT; peers use reported EBIT consistently.

Peer reported owner profits are TCS ₹49,210 crore, HCLTech ₹16,642 crore and Wipro ₹13,197.4 crore. Adjusted headline earnings differ. Wipro revenue is ₹92,624 crore; inherited ₹92,809.3 crore includes foreign-exchange income. Exact evidence and notes are retained in the snapshot.

## Before investment use

Research WACC, operating growth/margins and downside cases; expand or justify working capital; check lease consistency; assess restricted cash, reserves and investments; establish award dilution; import actual compatible prices/shares/bridges; and defend an investment thesis, catalysts and risks.

The implemented workflow makes these decisions reviewable. It does not approve them professionally.
